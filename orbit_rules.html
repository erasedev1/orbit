ORBIT

A two-player game of four concentric, rotating rings. Turn the heavens to trap your rival's moons — first to three captures wins.

2 players · ages 10+
8 moons each
10–20 min

The board

spoke Gold gate Cyan gate

Setup

Four rings hold 6 / 12 / 18 / 24 cells from the core outward — 60 in all. The six spokes are the angles where all four rings line up; everything radial happens on them.

Each player keeps 8 moons in reserve. Gold enters at the top and bottom gates; Cyan at the two side gates. The board starts empty.

Winning

3captures

Remove three enemy moons and the game is yours. If a time limit is used, most captures wins.

Your turn — take one action

  • Launch Place a moon from reserve onto one of your two gates on the outer ring, if that cell is empty.
  • Step Slide one moon one cell along its ring into an empty cell — or, only while it stands on a spoke, one ring inward or outward to the empty aligned cell.
  • Rotate & ride Turn any one ring a single notch; every moon on it travels, yours and theirs. You may then take one free follow-through step with one of your moons on that ring. You may not immediately reverse the opponent's last rotation.

Every rotation is a trade — it moves their moons too. Skill is finding the turn that helps you more than it helps them.

Capturing — only on the spokes

Trap a lone enemy moon between two of yours on the neighbouring rings of one spoke. Traps are checked after every action, in the mover's favour.
